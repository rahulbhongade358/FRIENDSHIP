Interlude

A letter from me

Dear M and S,

I honestly don’t know how to say this without smiling like a fool, so I thought I’d put it into words. ❤️

It’s been almost ten years, and somewhere in my heart, I’ve always carried the memories of our childhood — those silly little moments, the laughter, and the time we spent together. Life took us in different directions, and as the years passed, we lost touch. But those memories never really disappeared.

Meeting you both again after so long feels genuinely special. It’s strange in the nicest way — so much time has passed, we’ve all grown up, and yet reconnecting brings back a little piece of those old days. 🥹🤍

TThank you for remembering me and for making it possible for us to reconnect. You both were a special part of my childhood, and I’m really happy that we have a chance to be in each other’s lives again.

I know things can’t be exactly the same as they were ten years ago, and that’s okay. We have new lives, new experiences, and our own journeys now. I just hope we can start a new chapter of this friendship — with more conversations, laughter, random meetups, and beautiful memories along the way.

I don’t want us to lose touch again just because life gets busy. I’d love for us to stay connected and let this friendship grow naturally, one moment at a time.

Some childhood connections are too precious to forget. And honestly, I’m really glad life gave us another chance to reconnect. Here’s to old memories, new beginnings, and many more happy moments together. ❤️✨

Always yours,
R
